Subject: Contract Risk Summary — TechVendor Ltd. Agreement (GATE: FAIL)
Overall risk is High because the contract contains multiple P1 clauses, so the gate result is FAIL.
Critical finding 1: Section 5.1 irrevocably assigns all Customer Data to Vendor, and Section 5.2 lets Vendor commercialise it during and after the term without notice or compensation.
Critical finding 2: Section 6.2 makes Customer's liability to Vendor unlimited, and Section 3.2 binds Customer to a 12-month renewal unless written notice is given at least 7 days before term end.
Recommended action: Before signing, strike or rewrite Section 5.1 so Customer keeps ownership of its data, and negotiate a cap on Customer's liability in Section 6.2.
